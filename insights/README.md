🚢 Titanic Survival Analysis

This project is a simple exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the Titanic dataset. The goal is to understand what kinds of passengers were more likely to survive the disaster.

I focused on cleaning the data, exploring patterns, and visualizing relationships between different features like age, gender, passenger class, and family size.

📊 What I looked at
Who survived and who didn’t
How gender affected survival
The impact of passenger class (Pclass)
Whether age made a difference
How traveling alone or with family influenced survival
Relationships between different features using correlation

🛠 Tools used
Python
Pandas
Matplotlib
Seaborn

📈 Key insights
Women had a much higher survival rate compared to men
First-class passengers were more likely to survive than third-class passengers
Children had better chances of survival compared to adults
Passengers traveling alone had different survival patterns compared to those with family
Social class and gender were the strongest factors influencing survival
